Words I Never Said Aloud

Volume I

For everyone who felt everything but found no words — and for the ones who became the reason I found mine.

Free to Read
Part One

Love & Longing

Of stars, silences, and someone who became everything.

The Day I Found You

I wasn't looking for anyone that day
just another ride to college,
the same road, the same faces.
But then my eyes found you,
and in a single glance, something inside me shifted.

At first, I told myself it was nothing,
just an attraction, a passing moment.
But the days went on, and every time I saw you,
my face lit up without me even trying.

When we finally spoke after a few days,
I smiled in a way I never had before.
Every conversation felt like a little world of its own,
your presence carrying a warmth I didn't know I was missing.

I haven't found the courage to say it yet,
but someday, I hope I will.
And maybe by then, the stars will still have some ink left
to write a future with both our names.

You feel like warmth, like a home I've never known,
like the quiet after rain, my stillness in chaos, my peace.
I never saw this coming, but I’m glad it did,
because finding you feels like finding where I belong.

I hope you wake up smiling every day,
your biggest fan adores that smile more than words could ever say.

The Girl My Dreams Drew

I don’t know your story,
But I’ve seen your eyes long before today.
Not on screens or streets,
But in dreams that never seemed to fade.

Once a month, without fail,
You’d walk in quiet like a breeze,
No name, no past, just presence,
Like a whisper only my soul could seize.

I thought you were fiction,
A trick my sleep played on me.
But now, here you are real, breathing,
Exactly how my dreams said you’d be.

The universe has a strange sense of timing,
A rhythm only the stars understand.
But how did it whisper your name
Into my sleeping mind, like it was planned?

You weren’t someone I met,
You were someone I was shown
Drawn by dreams in soft outlines,
Before I even knew I was alone.

There was no message, no reason, no clue,
Just the feeling that I already knew you.
And now, across this strange reality,
I feel that same quiet gravity.

I hope you wake up smiling every day,
Like the way your dreams wake me up gently, without delay.
Because there’s someone out here, your biggest fan, by the way…
Who adores that smile more than words can say.

You feel like warmth, like a home I’ve never known,
Even though we’re strangers, even though I’m alone.
I never saw this coming, that’s true
But I’m damn glad I stumbled into you.

And if the stars ever let our paths collide,
Know I’ll be the one waiting, arms open wide.
To not just meet you but build something true,
A life, a love, a dream or two.

My Only Favourite

I’ve written so many letters for you,
that you have no idea about.
So many poems and songs,
you might or not get to listen.
And I still don’t know how to feel about that.

I’ve read countless stories,
written a number of poems,
yet none I’d ever call my favourite.
I listen music every day,
but no melody has stayed.
I’ve travelled various places,
but nowhere ever felt like home.

Because until now,
no one ever felt like someone I could like.
And then there was you
you became my only favourite,
My calm,
My stillness in chaos,
My peace.

I don’t know how to tell you I love you.
Maybe because those three words
could never carry everything I feel.
So maybe four can:
You are my universe.

And maybe that’s selfish,
but I hope no one ever admires you
the way I do.
Sometimes I think it’s foolish
to open up like this,
to write, to feel, to want.

Then I look at you,
and it all feels worth it,
even if loving you was going to war against the fate,
I’d still chose you.
And now, I’m imagining us,
years from now.
You sitting beside me,
sunlight spilling over your smile.
And me still looking at the same eyes,
I fell for many moons ago.

No Heaven But You

That evening
the last time I saw you,
I looked as long as my eyes could hold,
as if sight alone
could stop you from leaving.
But even eyes have their limits,
and love manages to flow beyond them.

Your laughter still echoes in my head,
like your words hanging in the air
like mist after rain.
Now, no words remain
to describe what you mean.
The only I can say is:
you are the eternal home for my soul.

You have no idea,
how your silence breaks me.
How your sadness,
turns me into tears.

I hope you remember,
there is someone
who always rushed to meet you,
someone who found every picture empty
without you in it,
someone who thought of you
like prayers think of gods,
someone who included you in his prayers.

Now I wish for no heaven,
no light divine.
Only this
to become the dust
beneath your feet.

You

Maybe it’s just another ordinary night,
Yet something feels different.
The quiet midnight, the breeze through the window,
Music humming low in my ears,
And you drifting through my thoughts.
Like a place my heart keeps returning.
Like a little piece of paradise.

I am visualising a dream stretching far beyond sight,
A place where we existed so perfectly,
That every single moment felt worth holding forever.
I wish I could write a poem that could hold everything I ever felt,
But some emotions refuse to turn to words.

My heart has already chosen you.
Maybe that’s why you feel like the paradise itself;
That I’ve been waiting for so long,
And maybe I’ll always wait;
For the day, we finally share the same home,
The same light, the same tomorrow.
I’ve found a woman stronger than the world itself,
Someone I can imagine building a family with someday.

Every future I imagine,
Somehow leads back to us.
But even then,
It feels unfinished
Like a melody waiting for that one note
That your name can only fill.

If fate ever brings us to the same door.
I’ll open it without a second thought,
We will let everyone watch quietly.
As two unfinished stories decided to complete it.

Until then…
I will always be choosing you,
In every silence, every moment,
In every version of the future I imagine.
Because you aren’t just a memory.
You are the final destination.

I’ll always be carrying you like a gentle echo,
Not heavy, not hurting.
A reminder that,
Somewhere, there’s a home I haven’t reached yet,
And its walls still spell your name.

The Curve of Your Smile

They say if you love someone, let them go,
but most days my fingers ache
to type a small “hey” or “I miss you.”

Yes, I loved you, but never found the courage to say it;
and now, with you beside someone else,
I turn whole sentences into silence.

Even the silence feels heavy without you,
not only in big moments,
but in the way peace thins
without your smile.

Yes, we argued,
but even our fights left roots,
turning anger into meaning,
and distance into longing.

Even in the noisiest places I hear your voice,
in a crowd, your face feels like calm in the storm.

I’m here, describing a love.
More than any words can say,
while every little things
resembles me of you.

I don’t know if time betrayed us,
or if we met too soon, too late
but I know I can never say goodbye
to that curve of your smile.

Letting you go is itself the most painful feeling
and yet I can’t.
I’m still here, waiting for the impossible
for the future we never wrote.

If I can make one promise, it’s this:
my eyes will never unlearn you,
as if you were the only home
I could return to.

Unsaid

I easily get jealous,
not because I doubt you,
as a result of caring more than words to explain.

When I love, I love deeply,
resulting a constant fear of losing you.
So when I see you laughing elsewhere,
my chest tightens heavily.

I don’t want to limit your freedom,
But I’m just scared.
Scared of being replaced,
scared of not being enough.

I know jealousy isn’t appealing,
So I try my best to keep it in.
However now and then my heart spills out,
Since I overthink, I worry, I love.

If I ever get jealous,
it’s only because you matter to me.
Because only imagining of losing you,
hurts more than words to explain.

To the One I Found Without Looking

Hello,
I don’t expect you to understand this,
But I’ve known you longer than time will ever admit.
Not through texts, not in passing glances—
But in sleep, where the soul forgets what is real and what isn’t.

You don’t know me,
But I’ve walked beside your shadow,
I’ve heard your laughter in places my body’s never been.
You were always just a dream
Until you weren’t.

Seeing you outside that space felt like déjà vu,
Like my memory said,
“Ah, there you are.”

I don’t know when we will talk.
But when message finds you
Know that someone out here
Recognized you before the world introduced you.
And maybe…
If stars still have some ink left,
We’ll write the rest of this dream—
Together.

More Than Words

I don’t really know how to rhyme,
But I do know—I wanna talk to you… all the time.
My heart felt something before my mind ever knew,
And now, it’s like the world softly echoes you.

Your smile? It slows the whole day down,
Like a quiet sunset over a sleepy town.
And if keeping you close means crossing stars and skies,
I’d do it all —no questions, no whys.

Even the silence feels right, when you are near,
Like hush of the nights when stars appear.
Somehow, just hearing your voice makes things right,
Like stars deciding to show up early each night.

So, I hope you wake up smiling everyday,
Because there’s someone out here —your biggest fan, by the way…
Who adores that smile more than words can say

A Love You'll Never Know

I showed you a part of me
No one had ever seen before.
For you, I became someone else,
Did things I’d never done before.

I loved you when you felt distant,
When your replies came slow.
You were slipping away,
Still, I didn’t let go.

I loved you in silence, loved you aloud,
When answers came late, when your heart was clouded in doubt.
When the space grew wide and you felt far,
Still, I held you like my favorite star.

Now you’re his,
And I’ve learned my place.
I won’t keep chasing
A losing race.

Maybe in another time,
You’ll be the one who finds me first.
Before this love
Turned into a curse.

Maybe then, I’d be your “hello,”
Not just the shadow you’ll never know.

Part Two

Pain & Growth

Of aches carried quietly, lessons written in loss.

The Ache I Carry

I don’t have a favorite color,
Neither a favorite poem,
Nor even a cherished book.
I write and read countless stories,
Yet none I’d call my own favorite.

I love music—
But no melody lingers in my soul.
I enjoy wandering out,
Yet no place feels like it holds my heart.

I speak to over a hundred faces each day,
Yet not one echoes in my mind.
I recognize thousands by face,
But none live close to my spirit.
I watch movies,
Yet never wait with breathless hope.
I eat every day,
Yet nothing ever sparks craving or comfort.

I celebrate festivals beneath lights and fireworks,
Yet feel no true warmth within.
I scroll through hundreds of frozen smiles,
But none ever make me pause with joy.

I laugh in conversations,
But rarely with my soul.
I travel beside others,
Yet return with the same hollow echo.

I meet hundreds—
Yet somehow, remain unseen.

It’s easy to say what I do,
But hard to hold myself together
When everything feels distant and dull.

That’s why—
If I ever tell you I like you,
Understand you’re a rare constellation in my sky.
You are truly close to my heart.

Because until now,
No one felt like someone I could like.
Because honestly—
I don’t like anything else.
You became my only favorite.

You are not just someone I like—
You became my calm,
My stillness in chaos,
My peace.

Learn, Leave, Begin

Not every path needs to circle back.
Stars still shine bright
Even after memory stops playing tricks.
No spell. No trance.
Just learned to walk… alone, and fine.

People?
They should be treated like chapters
not a whole damn book.
'Cause when we separated,
it wasn’t you I missed,
it was the feeling I loved, not the person.

Attachment? Bloody crap.
How do you unlove someone
you never gave permission to settle in your soul?
You can’t uncare.
The soul forgets logic.
And everything just starts holding you back.

And they say,
“Time heals.”
But what if time just teaches you to wear the pain better?
Smile with blood still fresh on my lips,
Laugh through the ache that never quits,
Scroll past their name like it doesn’t scream

You see
It’s not about forgetting them.
It’s about surviving the version of you
that existed only because they did.

Midnight’s quiet until my brain starts narrating.
Half-songs, half-memories,
full-blown flashbacks.
what I’ve been trying to erase
It all reminds you:
Unloving is fiction.
We just learn to carry people quieter.

But now?
We don’t even care.
Everything ended, just like it had to.
Let it.
We’ve forgotten maybe not everything,
but enough to walk forward.

So to anyone still stuck in the damn loop
stop messing around with the past.
You’re not meant to reread the same chapter
just because you liked a few lines.

New chapters don't write themselves.
Begin.
Even if it’s hard,
Even if your hands still shake when you let go.

The past?
Don’t carry it too far
Let it sit untouched. Let it rot if it must.
You don’t owe it a return trip.

And hey,
You’ll never know if all the city lights are green
unless you start the damn car and drive.
So go.

Time doesn’t heal
It just hands you a different kind of pain
and teaches you to hold it better.
Let the wounds speak,
Let them teach.
Don’t treat people like they’re everything
just because they once felt like it.

Learn from everyone
the ones who loved right,
and especially the ones who didn’t.
That’s how you know
who to become,
and who the hell not to.

The Rest I Hope For

I have imagined my funeral
more times than my wedding.
And I hope, when death comes for me,
it leans close and whispers,
“Now, come on — the fight is done.

You can rest.”
I hope it takes my hand like an old friend,
the one who’s seen me break quietly
and still stayed,
the one who knows why my bones feel older than my years.
I hope it tells me,
“You’ve cared and endured enough,
now let me carry the rest.”
And I hope its voice feels like rain
after a summer that forgot how to end.
I hope it promises a place where the noise stops,
where no names follow me,
no unfinished goodbyes pull me back,
no ghosts of my own choices
wait at the door.

And when it comes,
with the pride of me in its eyes,
I’ll welcome death,
shake its hand,
and pull it into an embrace so tight
that the act of leaving
feels like the very first breath here.
I hope it knows
I am not running toward darkness,
but toward the absence of ache.

And when it’s done,
I hope it leaves no monument,
no shadow, no trace
only a weightless space
where I once stood,
as if I was always meant
to be passing through.

Evolving, Not Ending

It started with a text,
and just a passing look.
No expectations, no plans,
just something quiet the universe wrote between the lines.

You weren’t someone I waited for,
you were someone I didn’t know I needed,
until I looked up one day
and realized you’d become part of me.

In your laugh, I found ease.
In your silence, a strange kind of peace.
You didn’t arrive with fireworks,
but somehow, you lit up parts of me I didn’t know were dark.

Then came that day,
the moment I helped you walk toward someone you truly wanted.
And I did it without hesitation…
but not without feeling.
It wasn’t loss,
it was something deeper,
a weight in the chest,
a tear I never expected.

Because I didn’t know I could feel that much,
didn’t know you had carved so deep into my life.
Not as a crush, not as a fantasy,
but as someone who mattered more than most ever will.

I cried, yeah.
Not because I was losing anything,
but because I realized how much you’d become home to me.
And home doesn’t always look like walls or people staying still—
Sometimes it’s the people we watch grow,
people we still cheer for from the side,
people we hold space for in our hearts, even as things change.

You’re still here.
Still my person.
Still the one who gets it when I don’t say a word.
Nothing broke. Nothing ended.
It just grew into something more real.

So this isn’t a goodbye.
Not a sacrifice.
Not blame. Not a burden.
Just love. Quiet, constant, stubborn love
the kind that stays,
no matter what direction we’re walking.

So no matter where life pulls us,
or who walks in and out of the frame.
You’ll always be one of the names
my heart doesn’t forget to whisper.
Not because you stayed in one role,
but because you meant enough to change shapes and still remain.

We’re not done.
We’re not fading.
We’re just evolving—
Still here.
Still strong.
Still us..

The Sun Also Leaves

The sky turned soft gold,
The streets slowly woke up.
Birds sang like usual,
The breeze gently moved the curtains.
And everything felt calm.
Except me.

Earlier, the mornings used to be a new chapter.
Now they’re just a blank page I stare at.

Once, I used to rise to your voice,
Now, it’s just the alarm
Ringing across walls that hold no echo back.
They used to be my beginning,
A spark in my stillness.
It used to be the mornings worth waking up to,
But now even the sun has forgotten how to warm my soul.

The tea has started tasting dull without you,
Now, the silence is louder than any chaos.
The birds still sing,
But your warmth, comfort and gentle wake-ups are gone.

This fine morning reminds me,
Peace was never the calmness.
It was your presence,
That filled the emptiness and made the silence feel alive.

तिमीलाई आफ्नो साथमा ठान्ने पागल म नै त रहेँ।

तर धिक्कार छ मलाई —
किनभने अन्त्यमा पछुताउने म नै रहेँ।

र केवल तिमीलाई चन्द्रमाको झलकको जस्तो समझने छु।

Part Three

Life & Philosophy

Of the world as it is, and as we wish it were.

The Grass is Greener on the Other Side

Children reach for the weight of years,
while the aged long back for days long past.
Homes filled with voices dream of empty roads,
while wanderers ache for their mother’s gate.

Hands without a lover’s touch
yearn for warmth they’ve never known,
while hearts bound by promises
long to breathe in their own quiet skin.

The wealthy crave a simpler life,
the poor dream of abundance.
One lies restless in bed, wishing to rise,
another staggers home, aching to fall.

A breathless chest clings to its final thread of air,
while lungs still full beg to let it go.
The lonely open their arms to anyone,
while the crowded long for a moment alone.

City lights hunger for the stillness of hills,
silent valleys dream of restless streets.
Travellers sleep in search of home’s comfort,
while those at home gaze at faraway skies.

Students write freedom into the margins,
workers scribble childhood on the same page.
The young pull hard against their leashes,
the old long for the gentle hands that held them.

Dreamers ache for ground beneath their feet,
the rooted secretly long for storms.
The night envies the sun’s warm breath,
the day aches for the night’s cool mystery.

Mountains gaze toward the ocean’s horizon,
the ocean envies the mountain’s still crown.
A flower dreams of the stone’s endurance,
a stone envies the flower’s fleeting bloom.

Silence wishes for a voice,
words beg to vanish into quiet.
Even time, ever moving forward,
turns its head to glimpse eternity.

The Quiet Wild

Sky turned soft gold
Birds started singing like usual
Breeze gently came and brushed the grasses
It didn’t stay long, neither made a sound,
but all the leaves turned to watch it around.

The sun poured gold where the shadows slept,
And rivers moved with no need to be kept.
Not a ripple asked where it had to go,
Just clear and wild in its own soft flow.

A silent place the world left behind,
No fences, no footsteps, just the wild
A beauty spot where nature even smiled.

Fate's Quite Magic

Started with a text, and one glance, no deeper thought,
Who would’ve guessed, what it was going to bring?
From strangers passing in a crowd,
To like stars aligned under the moon.

Known from months, not years,
Yet it feels like have shared ages together.
Felt like a fate, or maybe God’s plan,
Grateful that universe brushed us, hand to hand.

Feels like the stars had this plan all along,
You showed up softly but became so strong.
Not just a friend, something deeper, something rare,
Like family I chose, not just someone there.
Made so many memories in such a little time,
Silly jokes, small talks, dumb stuffs that somehow rhyme.
Hours of chats, but in person we freeze,
Yet even your silent presence puts me at ease.

You just get it, even when I say none,
Our bond feels like magic, like a setting sun.
Didn’t plan for this, but I’m so glad it came,
You gave this college life a whole new name.
Hope time doesn’t turn this into "remember when,"
Hope we annoy each other again and again.
Hope this grows like wine, better with years,
Becoming a story worth whispers and cheers.

We never saw this coming, true,
But I’m damn glad I stumbled into you.
You came in quiet, but stayed so loud,
Lifted my days, made me real proud.

Not every friendship makes this mark,
But yours lit fire in my dark.
Just grateful the universe did its thing,
And let our random start mean everything.

My Safe Place

Today, I Woke up with no alarm,
Just birds chirping outside,
The sun through curtains to my face,
And a dog barking far away.

Today felt like a memory that hasn’t happened yet.
You crossed my mind like the sunlight across the floor.
And this quiet morning feels a little less alone.

I don’t know what you’re doing right now,
Or if your eyes have even opened to this morning,
But knowing you’re breathing under the same sky
Made my tea taste a little warmer.

Maybe we won’t be talking today or the next day.
But friendship like this,
Doesn’t need a text or a call to be loud.

There’s a peace in this friendship,
A warmth like a home.
You’re beside me in my every struggle,
Even though you’re miles away.

You’re not just a friend to me,
You’re part of my family and my world.
My safe place, my home,
No matter the distance between us.

The First Thing I Ever Liked

I don’t have a favorite color,
Neither a favorite poem,
Nor even a cherished book.
I write and read countless stories,
Yet none I’d call my own favorite.

I love music—
But no melody lingers in my soul.
I enjoy wandering out,
Yet no place feels like it holds my heart.

I speak to over a hundred faces each day,
Yet not one echoes in my mind.
I recognize thousands by face,
But none live close to my spirit.
I watch movies,
Yet never wait with breathless hope.
I eat every day,
Yet nothing ever sparks craving or comfort.

I celebrate festivals beneath lights and fireworks,
Yet feel no true warmth within.
I scroll through hundreds of frozen smiles,
But none ever make me pause with joy.

I laugh in conversations,
But rarely with my soul.
I travel beside others,
Yet return with the same hollow echo.

I meet hundreds—
Yet somehow, remain unseen.

It’s easy to say what I do,
But hard to hold myself together
When everything feels distant and dull.

That’s why—
If I ever tell you I like you,
Understand you’re a rare constellation in my sky.
You are truly close to my heart.

Because until now,
No one felt like someone I could like.
Because honestly—
I don’t like anything else.
You became my only favorite.

You are not just someone I like—
You became my calm,
My stillness in chaos,
My peace.

आकाांक्षाको यात्रा

म अहिले आफ्नो विशाल आकांक्षाहरू पूरा गर्न हिँडिरहेको छु,
मेरा सपनाहरूमा काम गर्दैगैरहेको छु।
अब खाली हात बसेर हैन केही आँट लिएर,
आफ्नो उत्सुकताका उत्तर खोजेर अघि बढिरहेको छु।

समाजका विकृतिहरु बुझ्दैगैरहेको छु,
ती विकृतिहरुको जरो समातेर हटाउने प्रयास गरिरहेको छु।
बोझ्न नजाने जिब्रोहरूलाई कवर दिन खोजिरहेको छु,
मौन रहेर हैन, आफ्ना कुरा राखेर नयाँ भिडिँकिरहेको छु।

वरिपरिका ढोंगी नायकहरु पहिचान गर्दैगएको छु,
बिस्तारै त्यस्ता नायकहरुबाट टाढिँदैगएको छु।
आफ्नो ठाउँ आफै बनाउने सपना देखिरहेको छु,
ती सपना पूरा गर्न आटोट परिश्रम गर्दै ज्ञान बटुलिरहेको छु।

हरेक दिन रमाउँदै आफूमा काम गरिरहेको छु,
आफ्नो कलालाई प्रस्तुत गर्न सिकिरहेको छु।
हरेक क्षेत्रका बिषयमा ज्ञान लिँदैगैरहेको छु,
कल्पना र यथार्थको बीचको फरक बुझ्दैगएको छु।

मेरा चाहना

आकाशको बादल जस्तो फैलन चाहन्छु,
सपना र संघर्षका असीम बाटोहरू समेट्न चाहन्छु।
जसरी बादलहरू आकाशमा फैलिएर फैलन्छन्,
त्यसरी नै मेरा हर्षका भावनाहरु फैलाऊन चाहन्छु।

सूर्यको प्रकाश जस्तो उज्यालो चम्किन चाहन्छु,
तर त्यो चमकले केवल आफूलाई नभई,
पृथ्वीका सबै अंकुरलाई पखाल्न चाहन्छु।
जसरी सूर्यले रातको अन्धकारलाई चिर्दै दिन ल्याउँछ,
त्यसरी नै म यस संसारमा हर्षका पल ल्याउन चाहन्छु।

आशाको किरण जस्तो निरन्तर टल्किन चाहन्छु,
विश्वलाई जय र समानतासँग सजाउन चाहन्छु।
आफ्नो सत्यको आवाजलाई गुन्जाऊन चाहन्छु,
आफ्ना डर छाडी, हिम्मतले समाजका जन्जिरहरु तोड्न चाहन्छु।

आज भोली मात्र नभई सबार यही उमंगमा रमाउन चाहन्छु।
मेरो यो यात्रा सानो भए पनि,
संसारलाई एक नयाँ आशा दिन चाहन्छु,
मेरा विशाल आकांक्षाहरू पूरा गर्न चाहन्छु।

सपना र यथार्थ

धेरै ठूला ठूला चाहना बोकेर आफ्नो ठाउँ छाडेको,
आफ्ना सेतो चाहना पूरा गर्न भनेर अघि बढेको म।
पढ्न भनेर मनमा हजारौँ सपना जोडेर,
खाली हात नभएर केही आँट लिएर अघि बढेको म।

सोचेको थिएँ, यहाँ आएपछि नयाँ संसार खुल्नेछ,
तर बिस्तारै बुझ्दैछु की यो बाटो निकै कठिन रहेछ।
कहिले काहीं लाग्छ सम्भावनाको ढोका आफै खुल्ला,
तर वास्तविकतामा त्यत्तिको केही नरहेछ।

कक्षा कोठामा किताबको भार, मनमा प्रश्नहरूको लहर,
तर कहिल्यै जागेन दिमागमा पढ्ने रहर!
रातभरि पढ्ने संकल्प, बिहान अल्छीले नै रोक दियो,
लेख्न बसें, तर उत्तर आउनै दिमाग ब्लक भइदियो!

घरमा बा-आमाको आशा, आफैँलाई घर
बाहिर दुनियाँको दौड, मनमा अनेक विचार भर!
"यहाँसम्म आइपुगेको छु, अब अगाडि बढ्नैपर्छ" भन्ने कुरा छ,
तर कहिलेकाहीं लाग्छ के यो सपना पुरा हुन्छ त?

आफ्नै क्षमताथि प्रश्न उठ्यो, के म साँच्चै योग्य छु भनेर।
केही त बुझिएको छ कि म आफैँसंग झगडा गर्दैछु फेरि?
समय बित्दै जांछ, जिन्दगी रहर मात्र हैन, संघर्ष पनि हो,
यहाँ जित्न सक्नेले मात्र आफ्नो भविष्य लेख्ने हो।
