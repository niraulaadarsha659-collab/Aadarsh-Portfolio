About

Words, research, and service — built out of the same fire.

I'm Aadarsh Niraula. I completed my +2 (Grade 12) at Golden Gate International College. Since then, my work has moved across three lanes that all trace back to the same instinct: turning hard, lived experience into something that helps someone else.

I've written two poetry collections, Words I Never Said Aloud (Volumes I & II), and recently completed an independent research study on persona drift in AI role-play, formally published with a DOI. Alongside the writing and research, I've spent years as a social worker and activist — leading children's clubs, organizing community programs, and speaking at events during and after my school years.

I was also recognized as the Most Active Participant at a national CanSat satellite training organized by NAST and Antariksha Pratisthan Nepal, awarded the book The Young Satellite.

Research

Independent Research

Two published studies, spanning AI behavior and human genetics — different fields, same instinct for building a reproducible framework before drawing conclusions.

Persona Drift in Multi-Turn Character Role-Play

A Cross-Platform Evidence and Coding Framework. Six publicly available AI platforms — Claude, DeepSeek, GPT, Gemini, Grok, and Meta AI — were tested against a single fixed character specification and a fixed 15-turn narrative sequence, scored against a locked coding manual built before any scoring took place. The study measured not just whether drift happened, but how: as a single sharp contradiction, or a gradual slide across several turns.
