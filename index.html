01 · About

Three lanes, one instinct: make something useful out of experience.

I’m Aadarsh Niraula. I completed my +2 (Grade 12) at Golden Gate International College. My work now moves between writing, independent research, and community advocacy.

I’ve written two poetry collections, Words I Never Said Aloud (Volumes I & II), and published independent research spanning AI behavior, human genetics, student wellbeing, and education reform in Nepal.

Alongside that work, I have led children’s clubs, organized community programs, spoken at events, and continued youth and disability advocacy as an independent social worker and activist.
